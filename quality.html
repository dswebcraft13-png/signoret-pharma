Analytical Integrity

Quality engineered into every batch from the start.

At Signoret Pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd., quality is an active discipline integrated into every stage of pharmaceutical processing. Established since 2019 in Industrial Area Phase-9, Mohali, our operations follow thorough analytical procedures to support formulation consistency and product integrity.

Every formulation batch of Tablets, Capsules, Syrups, and Injections undergoes documented quality checks and standard evaluation before clearance for dispatch.

Quality Framework

Three Stages of Quality Verification

Documented quality checks at every stage of manufacturing.

⚗

1. Raw Material & Excipient Testing

Active pharmaceutical ingredients and excipients are inspected upon receipt to verify identity, condition, and documentation before release to manufacturing.

⚙

2. In-Process Manufacturing Checks

During compression, encapsulation, liquid filling, and sealing, technical staff perform continuous in-process checks to ensure batch specifications are met.

📊

3. Finished Batch Quality Review

Finished goods undergo final analytical evaluation, release verification, and packaging seal checks prior to warehouse dispatch.

Testing Dimensions

Quality Checks Conducted Across Dosage Forms

Quality parameters aligned with the physical and analytical requirements of each dosage form.

01

Tablets & Hard Capsules

Weight variation monitoring, disintegration checks, physical uniformity, and blister sealing inspection.

02

Syrups & Suspensions

Physical appearance, fill volume verification, pH testing, and leak-proof cap seal inspection.

03

Sterile Injections

Visual clarity inspection, container seal integrity, and documented batch inspection records.

04

Packaging Materials

Foil and film verification, leak checking, carton print legibility, and batch coding inspection.

Controlled Environments

Controlled Cleanrooms & Environmental Discipline

Our manufacturing areas in Mohali feature controlled environments designed to maintain clean working conditions suited for pharmaceutical formulations.

Standard operating procedures (SOPs), sanitization routines, and personnel discipline prevent cross-contamination across dosage production runs.
