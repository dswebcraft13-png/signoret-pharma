Privacy Policy

Transparency regarding how Signoret Pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd. collects, uses, and safeguards information submitted via our website.

1. Introduction

Signoret Pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "our", or "us"), located at Plot No. 556, Industrial Area Phase-9, Mohali, Punjab 160062, is committed to respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle personal details shared when contacting us or making enquiries through this website.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information voluntarily provided by you through our website enquiry forms, direct phone calls, or WhatsApp conversations. This may include:

  • Your name and company or business name.
  • Contact phone or mobile number.
  • Dosage requirements, product interests, or target district for PCD franchise distribution.
  • Any specific manufacturing or quotation requirements mentioned in your message.

3. Purpose of Information Use

Your details are saved solely to respond to your specific enquiry, provide requested manufacturing quotations, confirm franchise territory availability, or arrange technical consultations. We do not use your contact information for unsolicited spam or telemarketing.

4. Data Confidentiality & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business information to third parties. Information is stored securely on our backend database and accessed exclusively by authorized personnel of Signoret Pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd. for responding to business enquiries.

5. WhatsApp Communication

When you choose to continue an enquiry via WhatsApp, communication is subject to WhatsApp's standard end-to-end encrypted messaging terms and privacy policies.
